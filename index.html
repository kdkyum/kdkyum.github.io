Research

I am a postdoc in the Data Science for Humanity Group at the Max Planck Institute for Security and Privacy in Germany, working with Meeyoung Cha. I study how large language models encode knowledge in their parameters and activations, and how they decode it during inference.

Memory, unlearning, and editing

Localizing the parameters that hold a specific memory, and testing whether it can be truly erased or changed without damaging unrelated abilities, and whether the change generalizes beyond the targeted memory. (AI Engram, ICML 2026 Oral; Erase or Hide?, ICLR 2026; Moir, NeurIPS 2026)

Out-of-context reasoning

How models infer facts their training data implies but never states, without verbalizing their thoughts. For example, why models trained on “A is B” often fail to infer “B is A”, and which internal representations fix this. (Bilinear representation mitigates reversal curse, ICLR 2026)

Previously, I did my PhD in physics at KAIST, advised by Prof. Hawoong Jeong at the Complex Systems and Statistical Physics Lab, using deep learning to study nonequilibrium statistical physics.
